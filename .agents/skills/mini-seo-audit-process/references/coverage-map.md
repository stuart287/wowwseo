# Mini SEO Audit coverage map

Create this working map before writing. Keep it transient unless the client worklog is the agreed evidence store.

| Audit area | Required evidence | Destination | Completion check |
|---|---|---|---|
| Technical | crawl, indexation, redirects, performance, security/analytics scope | technical findings/actions | evidence and action agree |
| On-page | titles, meta, headings, media/alt text | on-page findings/actions | counts reconcile |
| Site structure | URLs, internal/external links, sitemap, orphan signals | linking/sitemap section | links and claims are traceable |
| Content | priority page samples, intent, length/correlation inputs | content-quality section | recommendations tie to pages |
| Keywords | keyword demand, intent, question set, gaps | keyword/content strategy | priorities have a rationale |
| Competitors | approved competitors, overlap/gaps, backlink profile, social proof | competitor section | no unrelated competitor facts |
| Summary | highest-impact actions and dependencies | conclusion/actions | no duplicate or generic claims |
