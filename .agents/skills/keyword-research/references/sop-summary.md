# Keyword Research SOP Summary

Source document:
- Google Doc: `https://docs.google.com/document/d/1uNlPz-XikuB9-8savrnrVSAh2OmJWNVIOjAwP-OOgIc/edit`

## Goal
Create a repeatable keyword research process for content creation that balances:
- search demand
- ranking potential
- search intent
- business relevance

## Core workflow
1. Copy the keyword research template and rename it for the client/topic.
2. Review the brief, strategy, content audit, goals, KPIs, audience, product/service positioning, and market.
3. Brainstorm seed terms.
4. Validate search intent with Google and early Ahrefs checks.
5. Expand the brainstorm list with:
- singular/plural variants
- synonyms
- spelling variants
- industry terminology
- intent-led modifiers
- comparative terms
- question terms
- brand terms where relevant
- seasonal modifiers where relevant
6. Export or gather Ahrefs data.
7. Filter and assess opportunities.
8. Review one or two SERPs more deeply when needed.
9. Attach the keyword research sheet during outline review.

## Seed term guidance
Include:
- informational modifiers
- commercial modifiers
- transactional modifiers
- service/solution modifiers
- feature-based modifiers
- use-case and audience modifiers

Examples from the SOP include:
- `what is`
- `how to`
- `guide`
- `best`
- `top`
- `review`
- `buy`
- `pricing`
- `vs`
- `alternatives`

## Intent matching
- Blog topics usually align with informational, comparison, or question-led intent.
- Landing pages usually align with commercial, transactional, service, or solution intent.
- Flag intent mismatches instead of forcing the wrong keyword onto the wrong page type.

## Ahrefs usage from the SOP
- Validate a few brainstormed terms first.
- Expand with related/matching keyword ideas.
- Review competitor URLs when useful.
- Review SERP examples for one or two high-priority keywords.

## Review mindset
- Choose keywords that support the content goal, not just the biggest volume.
- Watch for low-value or off-intent variants.
- Keep the final sheet decision-oriented and easy to hand off into outlining.
