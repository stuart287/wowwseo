# Optimisation Checklist

Use this as a practical audit guide when reviewing a page or producing recommendations.

## Keyword Research

- Keywords brainstormed and documented.
- Existing rankings reviewed for the URL where available.
- Ahrefs exports added to the keyword research sheet when supplied.
- Target keyword and focus keyphrase selected based on relevance, intent, and business value.
- Related terms and questions reviewed for section or FAQ opportunities.

## Ahrefs and Surfer

- Ahrefs Site Explorer used for existing URL rankings, ranking trends, parent topics, and keyword gaps when data is supplied.
- Surfer audit reviewed for content gaps, headings, term coverage, and competitor-aligned opportunities when data is supplied.
- Surfer terms treated as guidance rather than forced quotas.

## Metadata and SERP Presentation

- Meta title includes target keyword, matches intent, includes brand suffix where relevant, and stays within 60 characters.
- Meta description describes the page, includes a natural keyword, has a CTA, and stays around 120 to 140 characters.
- URL is concise, keyword-aware, and clear.

## Headings and Structure

- Page has one clear H1.
- Heading levels follow a logical hierarchy.
- Headings are descriptive, benefit-aware, and naturally keyword-informed.
- Sections are distinct and non-overlapping.

## Copy and Conversion

- Copy is clear, readable, specific, and commercially aware.
- Page explains the offer in an order that matches the reader's decision journey.
- CTAs are clear, action-oriented, and placed in natural decision points.
- Segues between sections support flow.
- Repetition, filler, and unsupported claims are removed.

## Internal Links

- Page links to relevant service, contact, about, case study, blog, or resource pages.
- Anchor text is descriptive and keyword-aware where natural.
- Opportunities for other site pages to link back to this page are identified.

## Images and Performance

- Image filenames are concise, descriptive, and keyword-aware where appropriate.
- Alt text describes the image accurately and avoids stuffing.
- Featured or social image metadata is checked when accessible.
- Speed recommendations are based only on supplied GTMetrix, PageSpeed, or similar data.

## Missing Sections

Consider recommending:

- Social proof, ratings, trust badges, or testimonials.
- Service detail sections.
- Benefits, USPs, guarantees, warranties, features, or pricing context.
- FAQs for objections or keyword-led questions.
- Blog or resource links.
- Stronger contact or final CTA sections.

Only recommend new sections when they improve clarity, trust, SEO coverage, or conversion support.
