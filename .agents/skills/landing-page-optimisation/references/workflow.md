# Landing Page Optimisation Workflow

## Operating Order

1. Identify the page being optimised.
2. Identify the client offering, audience, market, and goal.
3. Identify the target keyword or likely target keyword.
4. Identify source material: page copy, URL, brief, Ahrefs, Surfer, GSC, speed reports, and internal pages.
5. Organise the page into a section-by-section template.
6. Assess search intent and whether the page format matches the SERP.
7. Assess metadata, URL, headings, body copy, CTAs, images, internal links, FAQs, and section coverage.
8. Review supporting data when supplied.
9. Identify optimisation opportunities.
10. Present the output in a practical structure the user can apply.

## Search Intent

Assess content type, content format, and content angle.

For landing pages, confirm whether the SERP supports a service, product, commercial, transactional, or BOFU page. If results favour informational articles, comparisons, or guides, flag the mismatch and recommend either reframing the page, targeting a different keyword, or supporting the landing page with separate content.

## Metadata

Meta title:

- Include the target keyword naturally.
- Match search intent.
- Use an action-oriented word or phrase where relevant.
- Keep to 60 characters or less.
- Include the brand suffix where appropriate.
- Use the pattern `{action word} + {keyword aligned with company focus} + {| Site Title}` when it fits.

Meta description:

- Describe the page accurately.
- Include a relevant keyword naturally.
- Mention relevant services, benefits, or examples.
- Include a clear call to action.
- Aim for 120 to 140 characters.
- Match the brand tone.

URL:

- Keep concise and under roughly 60 characters.
- Include the target keyword where natural.
- Avoid unnecessary stop words.
- Reflect the page purpose clearly.

## Section Review

Assess each section for purpose, clarity, keyword fit, duplication, usefulness, conversion support, and whether it should be expanded, rewritten, moved, merged, or removed.

Common landing page sections:

- Hero
- Social proof
- Services
- Features, benefits, or USPs
- About or trust-building content
- Contact form
- Testimonials
- FAQ
- Final CTA
- Blog or resources section

Recommend missing sections only when they improve clarity, trust, SEO coverage, or conversion support.

## Copy, CTA, Links, FAQ, Images

Body copy:

- Improve clarity, readability, structure, flow, specificity, and commercial relevance.
- Use keywords naturally.
- Remove repetition and unsupported claims.
- Ensure the copy answers likely user questions and helps the reader take the next step.

CTAs:

- Review anchor text, placement, stage fit, and relevance.
- Suggest clearer, more action-oriented CTAs when useful.
- Add CTA-adjacent copy only where it improves conversion support.

Internal links:

- Suggest relevant service, contact, about, blog, or resource links.
- Prefer descriptive anchor text that includes relevant keywords naturally.
- Consider both outgoing links from the page and internal links the page should receive.

FAQs:

- Add FAQs for objections, common pre-sale questions, question-intent keywords, competitor gaps, or trust-building needs.
- Keep answers concise and non-repetitive.

Images:

- Assess filenames and alt text only when image data is supplied.
- Filenames should be concise, accurate, and keyword-aware where appropriate.
- Alt text should describe the image accurately and include a keyword only when natural.

## Surfer Keyword and NLP Term Handling

Interpret terms like `landing page optimisation 2/5-11` as current usage `2`, suggested minimum `5`, and suggested maximum `11`. Treat ranges as guidance, not hard quotas.

Prioritise terms in this order:

1. Primary keyword or focus keyphrase.
2. Close variants of the primary keyword.
3. High-relevance commercial or service-related supporting terms.
4. Question-based or objection-handling terms that fit FAQ sections.
5. Broader semantic terms that improve topic coverage.

Place important terms naturally in metadata, H1, key H2s or H3s, early body copy, service or feature sections, FAQs, CTA-adjacent copy, and image metadata only when they fit.

For landing pages, favour service-related, trust-building, and decision-support terms. Do not make a BOFU page read like a blog post just to include informational NLP terms.

When reporting Surfer terms, group them into buckets:

- Primary target terms
- Service or feature terms
- Trust or proof terms
- FAQ or objection-handling terms
- Secondary semantic terms

Before recommending a Surfer term, check whether it matches search intent, supports the business goal, fits the section naturally, and improves relevance without harming clarity.

## Missing Data Labels

Use clear labels when inputs are incomplete:

- `Provisional optimisation review`
- `Pending keyword research validation`
- `Pending Ahrefs review`
- `Pending Surfer audit review`
- `Pending speed / technical review`

Do not invent rankings, metrics, audit findings, image issues, or speed issues.
