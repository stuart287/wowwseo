# Keyword Research For Landing Pages

## Aim

Build keyword research that balances search demand, ranking potential, search intent, and business value for landing pages.

## Seed Term Discovery

Review briefing materials first:

- Client goals and KPIs
- Target audience and location
- Core products or services
- USPs and positioning
- Content audits or strategy documents
- Existing page purpose

Use Google search in incognito or a neutral environment to test whether terms return landing pages, product pages, service pages, guides, comparisons, or other formats.

Use People Also Ask, autocomplete, related searches, Ahrefs Keywords Explorer, and competitor URLs in Ahrefs Site Explorer to expand the term list.

## Brainstorming Coverage

Include relevant variations:

- Singular and plural forms
- Synonyms and closely related terms
- Spelling variations
- Industry-recognised terminology
- Intent-focused terms
- Comparative terms
- Question-based terms
- Brand-specific terms
- Seasonal or temporal terms
- Negative keywords where useful

Useful modifier groups:

- Informational: `what is`, `how to`, `guide`, `tips`, `ideas`, `benefits`, `checklist`, `tutorial`, `examples`
- Comparative: `vs`, `comparison`, `alternatives`, `better than`, `difference between`, `pros`, `cons`
- Commercial or transactional: `best`, `top`, `buy`, `cheap`, `affordable`, `online`, `near me`, `free trial`, `for sale`, `pricing`, `cost`, `review`
- Use case or audience: `beginners`, `small business`, `home`, `professionals`, `ecommerce`

For landing pages, prioritise commercial, transactional, service-focused, solution-focused, feature-focused, and BOFU variants over broad informational terms.

## Keyword Template Logic

The bundled keyword research spreadsheet has these tabs:

- `Existing keywords`
- `Brainstorming`
- `Overview`
- `matching`
- `related`
- `questions`
- `{keyword} - SERP`
- `{keyword 2 - SERP}`

The brainstorming tab combines modifiers and base phrases in both directions:

- Modifier plus base phrase, such as `affordable web design`.
- Base phrase plus modifier, such as `web design pricing`.

Flatten generated terms into export columns before copying them into Ahrefs.

## Ahrefs Export Process

Use the correct target region.

Export:

- Overview tab data
- Matching terms
- Questions
- Related terms
- SERP examples for one or two focus terms
- Existing URL rankings from Site Explorer when optimising an existing page

Use filters to narrow terms by keyword difficulty, volume, text includes or excludes, intent, parent topic, and relevance.

When reviewing SERP exports, consider domain rating, traffic, keyword count, backlinks, top keyword, parent topic, and rough word count. Verify competitor page content manually when decisions depend on content depth or format.

## Intent and Funnel Checks

Classify search intent as informational, navigational, commercial, or transactional.

Map likely funnel stage:

- TOFU: broad educational terms and problem-awareness queries.
- MOFU: category, solution, comparison, and evaluation terms.
- BOFU: product, service, pricing, location, brand, demo, consultation, or enquiry terms.

For landing pages, ensure the target keyword supports the page's commercial role. Informational terms can support body copy or FAQs, but should not distort the landing page into a blog post.

Do not rely completely on Ahrefs intent labels. Use manual SERP checks when the decision matters.
