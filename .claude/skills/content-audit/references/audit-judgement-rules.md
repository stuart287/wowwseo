# Audit Judgement Rules

These are working rules for consistent content audit decisions. Treat thresholds as guides, not laws; search intent and SERP expectations override mechanical scoring.

## Severity

Red:
- Important indexable pages have missing, duplicate, misleading, or unusable metadata.
- Pages target the wrong intent or multiple URLs compete for the same priority keyword.
- Important commercial or editorial pages are orphaned or receive very few internal links.
- Content is thin, outdated, untrustworthy, duplicated, or unlikely to satisfy the query.
- Site architecture makes priority content hard to discover or understand.

Orange:
- Metadata exists but is too long, too short, generic, boilerplate, or weakly differentiated.
- H1s/headings are present but poorly structured, unclear, duplicated, or not aligned with the query.
- Content covers the topic partially but misses key subtopics, examples, proof, CTAs, or user questions.
- Internal links exist but do not build clear topical relationships.
- Images exist but alt text, compression, or relevance needs improvement.

Green:
- The page satisfies intent, has clear metadata and headings, earns suitable internal support, and has enough useful content for the query.
- Minor polish may still be worth noting, but it should not distract from higher-impact work.

Blue:
- Use only for recommendations, next steps, or implementation notes.

## Metadata checks

Titles:
- Recommended range is usually about 55-60 characters, but SERP clarity matters more than exact length.
- Flag titles that are missing, duplicated, vague, too short to communicate value, too long to scan, or missing a sensible primary keyword.
- Check whether the brand suffix is helpful or crowding out the unique title.

Meta descriptions:
- Recommended range is usually about 100-140 characters.
- Flag missing, duplicate, overly long, overly short, generic, and boilerplate descriptions.
- Recommend descriptions that summarise the page, include the intent/topic, and give a reason to click.

Headings:
- Each indexable page should have one clear H1 in most cases.
- H1s should match page intent and introduce the topic naturally.
- Subheadings should make the page scannable and cover the user's likely questions.

## Content checks

Content length is diagnostic, not the recommendation by itself. If a page is short, identify missing information: comparisons, use cases, specifications, FAQs, evidence, examples, pricing, process, benefits, trust signals, or next steps.

Assess search intent first:
- Informational queries need clear answers, examples, definitions, steps, and related questions.
- Commercial investigation queries need comparisons, differentiators, proof, pricing or value context, and objections answered.
- Transactional queries need product/service clarity, trust, conversion paths, and supporting details.
- Navigational or branded queries need clarity, legitimacy, and fast routing.

Possible page actions:
- Keep: page is useful and performs its role.
- Improve: page has the right purpose but needs metadata, content, intent, media, or linking improvements.
- Consolidate: multiple pages overlap and split relevance.
- Redirect: a weak or redundant page has a stronger destination.
- Noindex: page is useful to users but not suitable for search.
- Remove: page has no user, search, or business value and no useful redirect destination.

## Linking checks

Internal links should support importance and topical relevance. Prioritise:
- Contextual links from relevant pages to priority commercial pages.
- Hub pages that connect related guides, services, categories, and supporting articles.
- Descriptive anchors that reflect the destination without forcing exact-match repetition.
- Fixes for orphan pages and high-value pages with unusually low inlink counts.

External links should support trust and usefulness. Flag irrelevant, broken, low-quality, excessive, or outdated destinations.

## Recommendation quality

Good recommendations name the page or page type, explain the SEO/business reason, cite the evidence, and describe the implementation.

Weak recommendations say only "add more content", "improve SEO", "optimise metadata", or "add internal links" without page-specific direction.
